Life outside work

Strong Outside, Steady Inside.

I never waited to feel ready. I started over, again and again, and each time it made me stronger.

Who I am

Adventurous, kind and empathetic, and honestly one of the happiest people I know.

Starting over

I moved from India to Canada to the USA for school and work, and since then across countries, states, places and people. Every move pushed me past my comfort zone and showed me I can overcome the challenges. Starting over, again and again, is my thing, and each time I came out stronger.

A birthday gift to myself

For my birthday, I booked a trip to Europe in two days, a surprise for myself, and packed the same day. I landed in Venice with two bags, found a hotel nearby, and figured out what to see in a foreign country. It was the best birthday I could have given myself. I learned I can always trust myself, take care of myself, and give myself the best experience ever.

Solo on purpose

I love exploring new places on my own, even dinner on a busy Friday night. It helps me grow.

Always trying

Rubik's cube in 90 seconds, pottery, painting, dance, and a flash mob I organized for 700 people at the office. Always open to something new, never the same thing twice.

Animals

Horses lately, but I love them all: horses, goats, elephants, you name it.

Food

Nothing beats my mom's cooking. After that, Italian seafood pasta is my go-to.
